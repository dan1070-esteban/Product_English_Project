Product Description

One button. One destination: your home.

RewindGo is programmed just once from the app: you save your home location as a fixed "rewind" point. From then on, you carry the remote with you wherever you go.

When you want to return, press the red REWIND button. The light ring and vibration guide you step by step, as if the path rewinds itself, until you reach your door.

01

Set your home as a fixed destination in the RewindGo app.

02

Go out peacefully and carry the remote in your pocket or keychain.

03

Press REWIND and follow the light arrow until you get home.
